About Meridian

A clearer way to plan a degree abroad

We built Meridian after watching too many strong applicants lose offers to paperwork, not merit. The platform exists to remove the friction between a good student and the right program.

Our approach

Three principles guide every decision we make

Verify before we list

No institution appears on Meridian until its accreditation and program details are independently confirmed.

One profile, every application

Your documents, transcripts, and history live in a single place — re-used, never re-typed.

Honest status, always

If an application is stalled, delayed, or rejected, you'll see exactly why — not a generic "pending".
